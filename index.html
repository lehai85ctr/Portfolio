Professional Summary

With over 17 years of distinguished experience in the construction industry, I have established myself as a results-driven Senior Construction Project Manager capable of leading and delivering complex, large-scale projects across multiple sectors. My career spans a diverse portfolio of commercial buildings, luxury residential developments, educational facilities, healthcare institutions, infrastructure projects, and industrial facilities — collectively representing billions of Vietnamese Dong in investment value.

Throughout my professional journey, I have developed comprehensive expertise in all phases of the construction project lifecycle — from initial feasibility studies and tender management through to construction execution, quality assurance, and final project handover. My approach integrates rigorous schedule control, proactive cost management, and steadfast commitment to quality and safety standards, ensuring that every project meets or exceeds stakeholder expectations.

As a construction leader, I excel at managing multidisciplinary teams of contractors, consultants, and subcontractors. I am proficient in industry-leading tools including BIM Coordination, AutoCAD, Primavera P6, and Microsoft Project, enabling data-driven decision-making and efficient project delivery. My strong background in contract administration, risk management, and quantity surveying ensures comprehensive project governance from inception to completion.

I am passionate about building sustainable, high-quality structures that create lasting value for communities and stakeholders alike. My commitment to continuous improvement, adoption of modern construction methodologies, and dedication to professional excellence have consistently resulted in successful project outcomes with an on-time completion rate of 98%.
